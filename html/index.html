PantryChef

Welcome!

PantryChef helps you turn whatever you’ve got in the kitchen into a real meal. List the ingredients you have — separated by commas — and we’ll generate a recipe that makes the most of them.

You can add a twist like “creamy”, “stir-fry”, or “spicy” to nudge the style.

Tip: more detail (spices, sauces, style) → smarter recipes. 🍳
